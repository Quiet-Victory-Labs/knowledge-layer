# Code of conduct

We want a respectful, technically rigorous community. Be considerate in issues, reviews, and discussions. Focus criticism on ideas and evidence, avoid personal attacks and harassment, and respect private customer and contributor information.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and limit participation when
needed. Report a conduct concern privately to
[hello@quietvictorylabs.com](mailto:hello@quietvictorylabs.com), with subject
`Knowledge Layer conduct concern`. Eric Kittelson is the current maintainer.
Use synthetic examples or redact private information. Inbox delivery and
monitoring verification remain part of the public launch checklist.
