## Remaining boundaries

Candidate pages and current-head presence checks are bounded to 256; exact term
matches/suggestions to twenty; source selections to eight; packets to 256 KiB and
receipts to 4 KiB. Capped search cannot establish complete recall. Requests that
exhaust the shared citation-operation budget must narrow their scope. Registry
checks cannot provide an atomic authorization lease across independent customer
systems, and previously delivered text cannot be recalled.

Exact inventory dependencies in company guidance, primary-concept RDF projection,
native formula compilation and implicit cross-source equivalence are not added.
Company guidance can already be scoped to a catalog connection without a snapshot
dependency. Native text is evidence, not a company instruction.

Real catalog APIs/ACLs, customer IdP/cloud deployment, representative estate-scale
latency/endurance, named-harness compatibility, and paired analyst-value holdouts
remain independent open qualification gates. These synthetic registry results do
not establish enterprise production readiness or a public release.
